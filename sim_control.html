Edit Mode

Tap a vital tile once to arm it. Three tools sit bottom-left of the graph: + Add drops the next point wherever you tap/drag ahead of "now" — applied the instant you release, ready for the next point right away.

Move lets you drag ANY existing point — including ones from the scenario's original build, not just ones you've added — to a new value or time, even past another point to reorder them. Remove deletes whichever point you tap (with a confirm).

Nothing before "now" is ever touched — only the future can change.

With no tool action in progress, tap the same tile again to open manual number entry instead. Turn Edit off to go back to tiles just toggling which lines are shown on the graph.
